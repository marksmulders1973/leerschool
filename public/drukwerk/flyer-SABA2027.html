Fifteen minutes a day — really understand what you learn.

Practise Dutch for free — for every child on Saba

Learning Dutch is easier when you practise a little bit every day. Leerkwartier ("quarter-hour of learning") is a free Dutch practice app made in the Netherlands: reading, maths and language exercises in simple Dutch, with short sentences and a read-aloud button. When a child gets a question wrong, the explanation becomes simpler — and then simpler again — until it clicks.

Shared with you by the Queen Wilhelmina Library: the exercises are in Dutch on purpose — that is the practice! You do not need to buy anything, make an account or install an app. It works right away, on any phone, tablet or (library) computer — Apple, Android or Windows.

What's inside?

  • Dutch reading practice that starts small (five sentences) and builds up step by step
  • Maths and language exercises for ages ± 6-14
  • Got it wrong? The explanation gets simpler, and then simpler again
  • A read-aloud button — hear how the Dutch words sound
  • A friendly learning buddy that helps without giving the answer away

Also without good internet

  • Printable workbooks with answer keys — practise on paper
  • Uses very little data; much of it works offline
  • Print everything at leerkwartier.app/printen

Not just for children

Anyone who wants to learn Dutch is welcome — parents, neighbours, the whole community. Short sentences, simple words and audio support make it a friendly start for adults too.

Fair and safe

  • The basics are free — guaranteed through 2031. No trial, no subscription, no payment details
  • No ads, no selling of data (GDPR-friendly)
  • Made by one dad in the Netherlands — not by a marketing machine
