## Tekst
Dear team of [ORGANISATIE],

In August I wrote to you about Leerkwartier, a Dutch practice app for primary school children. Since then we have built a part that may suit your pupils better: a free section for children who are learning Dutch as a new language.

Everything is in simple Dutch, and every sentence, question and answer can be tapped to see it in English. So children keep practising Dutch, but they are never stuck on a word they don't know. There are short parts about words in the classroom, maths words, counting to 20 and 100, letters and sounds, reading and times tables. A read-aloud button helps children who cannot read Dutch well yet. In the past month it was used on more than 60 devices.

It works directly in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer, also on the classroom board: no account, no ads. Practising is free, guaranteed through 2031.

Have a look, and choose "English" as the home language:
leerkwartier.app/nieuwkomers?utm_source=saba

Would this help in your classes or at home? I would be glad to hear what your pupils need most. A reply to this email is enough.
